## 🎨 Photography & Design Guidelines

### Gotham City (Dark, Moody)
**Time of Day**: Night, dusk, overcast, rain
**Keywords to Add**: noir, Art Deco, Gothic, 1940s, shadows, moody
**Color Grading**: Cool tones, deep blacks, amber streetlights
**Season**: Fall, winter preferred
**Weather**: Rain, fog, snow enhances mood

### Metropolis (Bright, Optimistic)
**Time of Day**: Golden hour, midday sun
**Keywords to Add**: modern, clean, futuristic, gleaming, bright
**Color Grading**: Warm tones, vibrant blues, high contrast
**Season**: Spring, summer preferred
**Weather**: Clear skies, sunshine

### Bludhaven (Industrial, Gritty)
**Time of Day**: Overcast day, industrial lighting
**Keywords to Add**: warehouse, industrial, working-class, docks
**Color Grading**: Desaturated, gritty, urban decay
**Season**: Any
**Weather**: Overcast, industrial atmosphere
